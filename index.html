Rider tips · सावधानी

Before you pay anyone anything

Check the address

Real government course sites end in .gov.in, .nic.in or .ac.in. PMKVY training is free. If an agent asks for a "registration fee" for a free scheme, walk away.

Learn free, pay for paper only

On NPTEL and SWAYAM you can watch every lecture and do every assignment free. The fee is only for the proctored exam, and only if you want the certificate.

Ask for financial aid

On Coursera, look for the "Financial aid available" link next to Enroll. Write honestly about your situation. If approved, the certificate costs nothing. How to apply ↗

No internet at home?

Swayam Prabha runs free education channels on DD Free Dish. ITIs, PMKVY centres and Jan Shikshan Sansthan teach in person. Download NCERT books once and read offline.

Get paid to learn

Apprenticeships under NAPS and NATS pay a monthly stipend while you train at a real company. It is often the fastest way from course to job.

Find scholarships

Central and state scholarships are listed in one place on the National Scholarship Portal ↗. Apply every year; deadlines usually fall in autumn.
